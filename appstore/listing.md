## Description

It is seven in the evening. The fridge is full and you still cannot answer the only question that
matters, so you order something and the spinach goes brown.

Larder answers it.

Scan a barcode or a shelf label and the pantry fills in. Larder reads what is actually at home,
notices what is about to go off, and names one dinner you can make tonight. It tells you the few
things you are missing and drops them straight onto a shopping list that is already open on your Mac.

WHAT IT DOES

Scan the shelf. Point the camera at a barcode. The first time, you type the name once. After that the
product is remembered and every later scan lands in one tap, with no network. Reading a whole label
works too: the words on the package become rows in the pantry, sorted by aisle.

Dinner from what you have. Larder runs a language model on the device, so it can read your actual
shelf and answer with an actual dish: the name, why it suits tonight, the minutes, the method, and
the shortest shopping trip if one is needed. Nothing is sent to a server. There is no account and no
requests leaving your phone.

A list that is already where you are. The pantry and the list live in your own iCloud. Check off
milk on the phone and it is gone on the Mac. Move the checked lines into the pantry when you get home
and tomorrow's dinner knows about them.

Words, not forms. Type "2 cans chickpeas, parsley, a lemon" and it becomes three rows with the right
aisles. Cooking something takes its ingredients off the shelf, so the next suggestion is honest.

Ask it out loud. "What is for dinner in Larder" works in Siri and in Shortcuts. Add to the list
without opening the app. A widget keeps the shelf count and the list on your Home Screen.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

No account. No analytics. No ads. No subscription. No cloud recipes, no social feed, no scanning your
receipts to sell you something. The model runs on the device, so it works on a train.

DETAILS

iOS 26 and macOS 26 or later. Dinner ideas need Apple Intelligence; the pantry, the scanning and the
list work without it. Larder is free of charge and free of tracking.
